Basis

The infrastructure for the neural-interface future is missing.

The long horizon is clear: neural interfaces and spatial computing converge until a person can plug into compute and live multiple lives, experiencing other embodiments, skills, and perspectives in high fidelity.

The differentiated, under-explored piece of that future is not the neuroscience. It is the infrastructure. Many neural and spatial streams have to be transported, synced, and kept consistent across a shared environment at scale. That is a distributed-systems problem.

Basis is an independent research lab treating the neural-interface future as an infrastructure problem. We are building the substrate that connects many human-like neural nodes into a single shared, real-time experience.

The future does not exist yet, but the infrastructure questions can be modeled today. We build with synthetic neural nodes and real open data, no hardware required: N synthetic brain nodes emitting realistic signals, connected through a shared environment and a consistency layer, instrumented end to end.

The stack is designed for AI agents as first-class operators, not just humans. Every component exposes a machine-readable interface so agents can provision nodes, tune the consistency and environment layers, read instrumentation, and act on it. AI is the primary way the stack is operated and improved.

We build the working stack first. Theory, experiments, and analysis layer on top of it, later.

The first project is Basis Nexus.
