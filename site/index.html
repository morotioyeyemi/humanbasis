The infrastructure for the neural future is missing.

The future of BCI (Brain-Compute Interfaces) and neurotechnology is unknown, and the timeline is uncertain. However far off it is, consumer BCI technology and spatial computing will likely converge on streams of human experience moving into and out of shared compute.

The under-explored piece of this future is not the neuroscience. It is the infrastructure. Every signal and bit must be transported, synchronized, and kept consistent across a shared environment at scale, within physical compute resources. This is a distributed systems problem.

At Human Basis we are doing independent, open research to answer (1) what does the future of BCI's intersection with spatial computing look like at scale and (2) what would be required, in terms of infrastructure and industry, to get there. For more on our thesis, what exactly we are doing, and why, please read: Biological Foundations.
